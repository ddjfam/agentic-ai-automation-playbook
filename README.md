## How I turn ideas into a delivery portfolio

1. Define the process problem, customer/user impact, volume and current effort.
2. Identify what can be eliminated, standardized, automated or supported by AI.
3. Prioritize value, readiness, risk and effort; assign a business owner.
4. Prototype with business experts and technology teams; test the failure paths.
5. Pilot with human accountability, measure results and scale reusable capabilities.

The **70/30 approach** is a workflow-design prompt: explore whether AI can absorb a larger share of routine effort so people can focus on judgment and exceptions. The allocation is a hypothesis to test for each process, not a fixed target or an achieved result.
