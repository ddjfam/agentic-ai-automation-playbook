# Wells Fargo: Enterprise AI Enablement

**Experience summary | Corporate Risk | Began in early 2026**

**Scale:** An organization of 10K+ employees across 21 business teams in the United States and overseas. I helped establish a four-person AI Enablement team. The team identified 20 major opportunities across the organization.

## Business problem

Business teams needed a consistent way to move from interest in AI to a well-defined process problem, a value case and a delivery decision. An idea alone did not explain the work involved, the data required or the change to the operating model.

## My contribution

- Helped stand up the enablement team and its intake approach.
- Built a web page to collect ideas, then built and integrated an intake agent.
- Used intake to help business leaders explain process pain, scale, effort and potential value.
- Screened ideas for large opportunities, practical quick wins and prioritization.
- Contributed hands-on to evidence preparation and readiness capabilities for a financial-crime AI initiative.

## Workflow redesign approach

I used a 70/30 discussion to challenge the current allocation of work: what routine gathering, checking and drafting could AI support, and what judgment, exceptions and accountability should remain with people?

The useful output is a redesigned workflow and a testable business case. The split is a planning heuristic, not a claim that 70% of work was automated.

## Delivered and still to be measured

**Established:** A small enablement team, intake capability and a pipeline of **20 major opportunities** identified across business teams.

**Individual hands-on example:** The [financial-crime readiness case](financial-crime-readiness-case.md) connects a large operating problem to a working prototype and reusable checks.

Opportunity identification is an early portfolio outcome. Implementation, adoption and realized benefit must be measured for each approved initiative.

## Reusable portfolio discipline

For a new CoE, I would track each idea through qualification, baseline, design, pilot and scale. Every stage needs a business owner, evidence, an explicit next decision and a reason to proceed or stop. The [intake framework](https://github.com/ddjfam/process-engineering-playbook/blob/main/intake-and-prioritization.md) is a proposed template for doing that; its scoring weights are not presented as the historical Wells Fargo method.
