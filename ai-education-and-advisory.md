## What I can help teams learn

- **AI fundamentals:** explain capabilities, limitations and appropriate uses in plain language.
- **Prompt design:** define the task, context, source material, constraints and expected output.
- **Workflow redesign:** find repetitive effort and identify decisions that require human judgment.
- **Responsible use:** protect sensitive information, check evidence and follow applicable approval requirements.
- **Agent concepts:** explain inputs, instructions, tools, exceptions and human review.
- **Business value:** establish a baseline and assess quality, effort and adoption before scaling.

## Illustrative employee workshop

*Proposed teaching format, newly written for this public portfolio; not an employer or OLLU course.*

| Step | Learning activity | Evidence of learning |
|---|---|---|
| Frame the problem | Select a routine task and define a useful outcome | Clear problem statement and baseline |
| Demonstrate | Walk through a fictional example with approved tools | Learner explains the task and AI's role |
| Practice | Draft and improve a prompt using synthetic information | A usable prompt and reviewed output |
| Challenge | Introduce an unsupported claim, missing context or sensitive-data concern | Learner identifies the issue and explains the next action |
| Apply | Define a small workplace experiment and required reviews | Owner, success measure and follow-up plan |

Suggested evaluation: a short knowledge check, an observed practice task and a follow-up review of whether learning transferred to work. Completion alone does not establish competence or business benefit.
